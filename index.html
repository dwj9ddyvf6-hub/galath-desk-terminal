All 15 systems · evidence

System scorecards

Each test picks its best variant on 2016-mid 2022 data only, then is judged on the later out-of-sample years (mid 2022-2026). Edge = average excess return per trade vs a same-day control of similar volatility, in %, with its 95% range. Candidate = the out-of-sample range sits above zero. The bars show that range against zero.

Paper trading, not investment advice. Simulated money on a broker paper account. Not a recommendation to buy or sell anything.
